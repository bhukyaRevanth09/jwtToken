# Nexora — Work From Home (WFH) Policy

## 1. Eligibility

- Available to all **confirmed employees** (i.e., those who have completed their 3-month probation) whose role doesn't require mandatory on-site presence.
- **Excluded roles:** facilities management, front-desk/reception, hardware-lab, IT infrastructure support requiring physical server-room access, and any role explicitly flagged "On-site Only" in the job description.
- **New joiners** work from office for their first 3 months (probation period) to support onboarding, buddy interactions, and team integration — no WFH days are granted during this period except for approved medical or emergency exceptions.
- Contractors and interns follow the WFH terms specified in their individual contracts, which may differ from this policy.
- Eligibility is reviewed automatically at the time of probation confirmation; HRBPs update the HRMS system to reflect hybrid-eligible status.

## 2. WFH Model

- Nexora follows a **hybrid model**: employees may work from home for **up to 2 days per week**.
- **Tuesday and Wednesday** are designated **"Team Anchor Days"** — all teams are expected in-office on these two days for collaboration, sprint planning, and cross-functional syncs. Anchor Days are org-wide and not subject to team-level override.
- The remaining 2 WFH days can be chosen flexibly from **Monday, Thursday, or Friday**, based on team and manager alignment — teams are encouraged to stagger WFH days so at least 50% of the team is in-office on any given day.
- WFH days do not carry over week to week; unused WFH days in a given week cannot be "banked" for a future week.
- **Full-week WFH** (e.g., due to relocation, medical reasons, caregiving responsibilities, or a family emergency) may be approved by the manager and HRBP jointly, on a case-by-case, time-bound basis (typically reviewed every 4–6 weeks). This is explicitly **not a standing entitlement** and can be revoked if business needs change or performance concerns arise.
- Managers may temporarily suspend WFH flexibility for a team during critical periods (e.g., a product launch, audit, or major incident), with advance notice of at least 3 working days where possible.

### 2.1 Requesting a Permanent Hybrid Schedule Change
- Employees wishing to shift their fixed WFH days (e.g., from Monday/Thursday to Thursday/Friday) can request this via the HR portal; changes take effect from the following week once approved by the manager.

## 3. Expectations on WFH Days

- Be available and responsive during **core hours (10:30 AM – 4:30 PM)**, identical to expectations on in-office days.
- Maintain a stable internet connection and a quiet, professional workspace suitable for video calls — background noise or unstable connectivity during client-facing calls may result in a manager conversation.
- **Attendance marking:** log in on the HR app at the start of the day and log out at the end of the day; missed attendance markings for more than 2 days in a month trigger an automated reminder to the employee and manager.
- Employees are expected to attend all scheduled meetings on video (camera on, where the meeting norm requires it) unless otherwise agreed with the team.
- WFH is not a substitute for leave — employees who are unwell or unable to work should apply for sick leave rather than "working from home" in name only.

## 4. Equipment & Reimbursement

- **One-time WFH setup allowance:** ₹15,000, for essentials such as a desk, chair, monitor, or keyboard/mouse — available once per employee upon confirmation as hybrid-eligible. Claims are submitted via the HR portal with receipts within 60 days of confirmation.
- **Monthly internet reimbursement:** ₹1,000, submitted along with the utility/ISP bill through the expense portal; unclaimed months do not accumulate or carry forward.
- **Company assets:** laptop and any issued peripherals (monitor, dock, headset) remain company property at all times and must be returned in working condition at the time of separation, internal transfer to a non-eligible role, or if the employee is placed on long-term leave exceeding 60 days.
- Loss or damage to company equipment while working from home should be reported to IT within 24 hours; repair or replacement costs may be recovered from the employee in cases of gross negligence, per the IT Asset Policy.

## 5. Requesting WFH Outside the Standard Policy

- Occasional WFH beyond the standard 2 days/week (for example, a one-off additional day due to a home repair or a delivery) can be requested via the HR portal, subject to manager approval, and **does not count against leave balance**.
- Such ad-hoc requests should ideally be raised at least 1 working day in advance, except in genuine emergencies.
- Repeated ad-hoc WFH requests (more than 4 in a rolling month) may prompt a conversation with the manager to reassess whether the employee's fixed WFH days need adjustment.
- Ad-hoc WFH on a Team Anchor Day (Tuesday/Wednesday) requires additional sign-off from the manager and is discouraged except for genuine one-off circumstances.

## 6. Non-Compliance

- Consistent disregard for Anchor Day attendance, core-hour availability, or attendance-marking requirements may be addressed through an informal conversation in the first instance, escalating to a formal performance conversation if unresolved.
- Misuse of WFH status (e.g., marking attendance without being reachable) is treated as a conduct issue under the Code of Conduct policy.
