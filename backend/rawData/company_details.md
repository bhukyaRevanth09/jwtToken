# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Org Structure, Roles & Active Projects

_Internal reference document — People & Operations_
_Last updated: January 2026_

## 1. Company Overview

Nexora Technologies is organized into five functions, each led by a Function Head reporting to the leadership team (CEO, CTO, and CPO):

| Function | Head | Approx. Headcount |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering & Product | VP of Engineering | 210 |
| Design | Head of Design | 28 |
| Sales & Customer Success | VP of Sales | 65 |
| People & Operations | Head of HR | 22 |
| Finance & Legal | CFO | 18 |

Total headcount: ~343 employees across Hyderabad (HQ), Bengaluru, and Pune offices, plus a small remote-first cohort (~15 employees) in other Indian cities.

## 3. Active Projects (Engineering & Product)

### 3.1 Project Atlas — Core Billing Platform Revamp
- **Goal:** Replace the legacy monolithic billing system with a modular, event-driven architecture.
- **Team:** 1 Engineering Manager, 2 Staff/Senior Engineers, 6 SDE-1/2s, 2 QA Engineers, 1 Product Manager.
- **Status:** Phase 2 of 3 — invoicing module in production pilot with 5 beta customers; subscription-management module in active development.
- **Tech stack:** Node.js (NestJS), PostgreSQL, Kafka, deployed on AWS (ECS + RDS).
- **Target completion:** Q3 2026.

### 3.2 Project Horizon — Customer Analytics Dashboard
- **Goal:** A self-serve analytics dashboard giving customers usage insights and custom report building.
- **Team:** 1 Engineering Manager, 4 Full-Stack Developers, 1 Data Engineer, 1 Product Designer, 1 Product Manager.
- **Status:** MVP shipped to all customers in December 2025; current sprint focused on custom report builder and export-to-CSV/PDF.
- **Tech stack:** React, Next.js, GraphQL, ClickHouse for analytics storage.
- **Target completion:** Rolling — quarterly feature releases.

### 3.3 Project Beacon — Mobile App (iOS/Android)
- **Goal:** Native mobile companion app for on-the-go account management, launched as a new market expansion initiative.
- **Team:** 1 Engineering Manager, 3 Mobile Engineers (React Native), 1 Backend Developer, 1 QA Engineer, 1 Product Designer.
- **Status:** Closed beta with ~200 users; targeting public App Store/Play Store launch.
- **Tech stack:** React Native, Node.js backend services (shared with Horizon's API layer).
- **Target completion:** Public launch targeted Q2 2026.

### 3.4 Project Sentinel — Internal Security & Compliance Tooling
- **Goal:** Internal tooling for access-review automation, audit-log aggregation, and SOC 2 compliance readiness.
- **Team:** 1 Staff Engineer (lead), 2 DevOps/Platform Engineers, 1 SDE-2, part-time support from Legal.
- **Status:** Access-review automation live internally; audit-log aggregation in progress ahead of the planned SOC 2 Type II audit.
- **Tech stack:** Python, Terraform, AWS CloudTrail/GuardDuty integrations.
- **Target completion:** SOC 2 audit readiness targeted end of Q3 2026.

### 3.5 Project Compass — AI-Assisted Support Ticket Routing
- **Goal:** Use a classification model to auto-tag and route inbound support tickets to the right team, reducing first-response time.
- **Team:** 1 Data Engineer, 2 SDE-2s, 1 Product Manager, close collaboration with the Customer Success team for labeling/feedback loops.
- **Status:** Pilot running on ~30% of inbound ticket volume; accuracy being tuned before full rollout.
- **Tech stack:** Python, a fine-tuned classification model, integrated into the existing support-ticketing tool via API.
- **Target completion:** Full rollout targeted Q2 2026, pending pilot accuracy thresholds being met.
