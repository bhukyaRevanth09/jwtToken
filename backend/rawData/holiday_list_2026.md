# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Holiday List 2026

Nexora Technologies observes **11 fixed holidays** (mandatory, company-wide) plus **2 restricted holidays** of the employee's choice, selected from the list below. This calendar is published annually by the HR & Facilities team and applies to all India-based employees unless a location-specific or role-specific variant applies (see Section 4 and the Leave Policy's role-wise holiday tables).

## 1. Fixed Holidays

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 1, 2026 | Thursday | New Year's Day |
| Jan 26, 2026 | Monday | Republic Day |
| Mar 4, 2026 | Wednesday | Holi |
| Apr 3, 2026 | Friday | Good Friday |
| Aug 15, 2026 | Saturday | Independence Day |
| Sep 14, 2026 | Monday | Ganesh Chaturthi |
| Oct 2, 2026 | Friday | Gandhi Jayanti |
| Oct 20, 2026 | Tuesday | Dussehra (Vijayadashami) |
| Nov 8, 2026 | Sunday | Diwali (Deepavali) |
| Nov 9, 2026 | Monday | Diwali — Compensatory Holiday* |
| Dec 25, 2026 | Friday | Christmas |

\*Diwali falls on a Sunday in 2026, so HR has declared Monday, Nov 9 as a compensatory holiday so the festival is observed on a working day. This is the only date-shift exception in the 2026 calendar.

### 1.1 Saturday Holidays
- **Independence Day (Aug 15, 2026)** falls on a Saturday. Per standard policy, holidays falling on a weekend are **not** automatically compensated with an alternate working day off, except where explicitly noted (as with Diwali above). Employees for whom Saturday is a working day per their team's work-week policy will not receive a substitute holiday for Independence Day.

## 2. Restricted Holidays (choose any 2)

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 14, 2026 | Wednesday | Makar Sankranti / Pongal |
| Mar 19, 2026 | Thursday | Ugadi |
| Aug 28, 2026 | Friday | Raksha Bandhan |
| Oct 29, 2026 | Thursday | Karva Chauth |
| Nov 24, 2026 | Tuesday | Guru Nanak Jayanti |

- Employees must inform HR of their 2 chosen restricted holidays **by January 31, 2026**, via the HR portal.
- Once selected, choices **cannot be changed** during the year, except in cases of a genuine documented error, correctable only within 7 days of the original submission.
- Employees who miss the January 31 deadline default to no restricted holidays for the year unless HR grants a documented exception (e.g., new joiners onboarding after January 31 are given 15 days from their joining date to select).
- Restricted holidays are only available to confirmed employees; employees on probation follow the fixed holiday list only (per the Leave Policy).
- Restricted holiday days do not carry forward to the next leave year and lapse if unused.

## 3. Compensatory Off for Working on a Holiday
- Employees required to work on a fixed or chosen restricted holiday (e.g., production support, client escalation coverage, event staffing) are eligible for a Compensatory Off, as governed by Section 2 of the Leave Policy.
- The manager must tag the worked holiday in the HR system within 5 working days for the Comp-Off to be validly credited.

## 4. Regional & Role-Specific Variations
- **State-specific holidays:** Offices in certain states may observe an additional 1–2 state-mandated holidays (e.g., a regional harvest festival or state formation day) not listed in the pan-India calendar above; these are communicated separately by the local HR/Facilities team for that office.
- **Role-specific calendars:** Certain functions (e.g., Design, Client-Facing/Customer Success, Facilities & Security) follow adjusted fixed/restricted holiday counts as detailed in the Leave Policy, reflecting differing operational coverage needs.
- **Client-site deployment:** Employees stationed at a client site should follow whichever calendar — Nexora's or the client's — results in fewer working-day conflicts for the engagement, as agreed between the account/delivery manager and HR.

## 5. General Notes
- Dates are based on the Government of India gazetted calendar and may be revised if the government notifies a change — this is most common for Islamic and lunar-calendar holidays (e.g., Good Friday, Eid-related observances where applicable) and can shift by a day depending on moon sighting or local gazette notifications.
- Any mid-year revision to a gazetted date will be communicated by HR at least 2 weeks in advance where possible, with the updated calendar re-published on the HR portal.
- Holidays falling on a Saturday or Sunday are not automatically compensated unless stated otherwise in this document.
- This calendar is reviewed and re-published every year by **December 1** for the upcoming calendar year.
