# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Leave Policy

_Applicable to all full-time employees of Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd._

## 1. Leave Year

- The leave year runs from **January 1 to December 31**.
- Leave balances are reset/re-credited on January 1 each year, subject to the carry-forward rules below.
- Employees joining mid-year accrue leave pro-rata from their date of joining (see individual leave type sections).

## 2. Types of Leave

| Leave Type | Days/Year | Carry Forward | Encashment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual / Earned Leave (EL) | 18 | Up to 30 days | Balance above 15 days encashable at year-end |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 6 | Not allowed — lapses on Dec 31 | Not allowed |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 6 | Up to 15 days | Not allowed |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks (first 2 children), 12 weeks (3rd child onward) | N/A | N/A |
| Paternity Leave | 10 working days, within 3 months of childbirth | N/A | N/A |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 days (immediate family) | N/A | N/A |
| Compensatory Off | 1 day per holiday/weekend worked | Avail within 30 days | Not allowed |

### Annual / Earned Leave (EL)
- Credited monthly at 1.5 days/month (18 days/year).
- New joiners accrue leave pro-rata from their date of joining — e.g., an employee joining on July 1 accrues EL from July onward only.
- Requires manager approval and should ideally be applied at least 3 working days in advance; leave during team-critical periods (release weeks, audits) may require additional lead time as communicated by the manager.
- A maximum of 15 consecutive EL days can be taken at a stretch without additional Business Unit Head sign-off.
- EL balance above 15 days at year-end is encashable at the employee's last drawn basic pay; balance up to 30 days can be carried forward, and any excess beyond 30 days lapses.

### Casual Leave (CL)
- Intended for short, often unplanned absences.
- Can be applied on the same day, subject to manager approval (retroactive approval permitted for genuine same-day needs).
- A maximum of 3 consecutive CL days is allowed before it must be converted to EL.
- CL cannot be combined with a public holiday to extend a break beyond the 3-consecutive-day cap without manager sign-off.

### Sick Leave (SL)
- Up to 2 days can be self-certified via the HR portal.
- 3 or more consecutive sick days require a medical certificate submitted within 3 days of returning to work.
- Frequent short SL patterns (e.g., single-day SL immediately before/after a weekend, recurring monthly) may be flagged by HR for a wellness check-in conversation — not disciplinary by default.

### Maternity Leave
- 26 weeks for the first 2 children; 12 weeks from the 3rd child onward, per applicable law.
- Can be availed up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.
- Employees may request an extension of unpaid leave beyond the statutory period, subject to manager and HR approval, up to a maximum of 3 additional months.
- A creche/childcare reimbursement benefit and phased return-to-work option (part-time for the first 4 weeks post-return) are available on request via HR.

### Paternity Leave
- 10 working days, to be availed within 3 months of childbirth (or legal adoption).
- Can be split into a maximum of 2 blocks within the eligibility window.

### Bereavement Leave
- 5 days for the passing of immediate family (spouse, parent, child, sibling).
- 2 days for extended family (grandparent, parent-in-law, sibling-in-law), subject to manager discretion.
- Does not require advance notice; documentation is not mandatory but may be requested for record-keeping.

### Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- Earned at 1 day per holiday or weekend day worked, applicable for roles required to work on a declared holiday/weekend (e.g., production support, on-call rotations, event-based work).
- Must be availed within 30 days of earning; unavailed comp-off lapses and is not encashed.
- Comp-off requires prior tagging of the worked day by the manager in the HR system to be valid.

## 3. How to Apply

- All leave (except emergency SL) is applied through the HR portal and requires manager approval.
- Emergency leave should be communicated to your manager and HRBP as early as possible (call, message, or email), with the formal application filed on return within 2 working days.
- Leave requests overlapping a public holiday or Team Anchor Day (per the WFH Policy) may prompt a manager query but are not automatically rejected.
- Approved leave reflects in the HRMS calendar and syncs with the employee's work calendar to auto-decline conflicting meeting invites.

## 4. Unauthorized Absence

- Absence without approval or intimation for 3 or more consecutive working days may be treated as **voluntary abandonment of service**, per company policy, and can lead to termination after due process (HR notice and opportunity to respond).
- A single unapproved absence is addressed informally by the manager in the first instance; repeated instances are escalated to HR.

## 5. Public Holidays

- Public holidays are listed separately in the **Holiday List** (below) and are not counted against any leave balance.
- The Holiday List is published annually by December of the preceding year and may vary by work location (state-specific holidays) and by role/function, as detailed below.
- Employees required to work on a declared public holiday (see role-wise lists) are eligible for Compensatory Off as described in Section 2.
