# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Benefits Policy

Nexora Technologies provides the following benefits to all confirmed, full-time employees. Most benefits are effective from date of joining; a few begin only after confirmation — noted against each benefit below. Contractors, interns, and consultants are not covered under this policy unless explicitly stated in their engagement contract.

## 1. Health Insurance (Group Mediclaim)

- **Coverage:** Employee + spouse + up to 2 dependent children, sum insured **₹5,00,000** per family, on a floater basis (i.e., the sum insured is shared across all covered members rather than allocated per person).
- **Parents' cover (optional):** Additional ₹3,00,000 floater cover for parents or parents-in-law (not both simultaneously — the employee selects one set at enrollment), available at a subsidized employee co-pay deducted monthly from salary. Enrollment is only during onboarding or the annual open-enrollment window (typically held in April); mid-year opt-in is not permitted except for a qualifying life event (marriage, birth of a child).
- **Pre-existing conditions:** Covered from Day 1 — no waiting period, unlike many standard individual health policies.
- **Maternity cover:** Up to ₹50,000 for normal delivery and ₹75,000 for a C-section, within the family floater sum insured; a 9-month waiting period from policy start applies for a first-time enrollee's maternity claim, waived for employees continuously covered under a previous employer's group policy with no break (subject to proof).
- **Network:** Cashless treatment at 8,500+ network hospitals across India via the empanelled Third-Party Administrator (TPA). Reimbursement claims for non-network or emergency out-of-network treatment are processed within 15 working days of complete documentation being submitted.
- **Effective from:** Date of joining. Insurance cards (physical and digital/e-card) are issued within 15 working days of onboarding; coverage is retroactive to Day 1 even before the card arrives — employees can use the TPA helpline with their employee ID for cashless admission in the interim.
- **Dependent addition/removal:** Can be updated within 30 days of a qualifying event (marriage, childbirth, adoption); additions outside this window are held for the next open-enrollment cycle.

## 2. Group Personal Accident & Term Life Insurance

- **Personal Accident Cover:** ₹10,00,000 — covers accidental death and permanent total/partial disability, 24x7 (on and off duty), including while commuting and during personal travel.
- **Term Life Cover:** 3x annual fixed CTC (minimum ₹15,00,000), payable to your nominee in case of death during active employment, regardless of cause (subject to standard life-insurance exclusions such as suicide within the first 12 months, per the insurer's policy wording).
- **Nomination:** Employees must submit nominee details at onboarding via the HR portal; nomination can be updated at any time and is strongly recommended after major life events (marriage, birth of a child).
- Both covers cease on the last working day of employment, with no continuation option offered by Nexora (employees may independently port to an individual policy with the insurer, subject to the insurer's terms).

## 3. Provident Fund (PF) & Gratuity

- **PF:** 12% of basic salary contributed by both employee and employer monthly, as per the EPF Act, 1952; employees may additionally opt for Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF) contributions above the mandatory 12%, deducted from salary.
- **Gratuity:** As per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 — payable after 5 years of continuous service, calculated at 15 days' wages (last drawn basic + DA) per year of service, capped at the statutory ceiling in force at the time of payout.
- **UAN & Portability:** Universal Account Number (UAN) details are shared at onboarding; employees are encouraged to link their UAN to Aadhaar for seamless PF transfer between employers.

## 4. Other Benefits

- **Leave Travel Allowance (LTA):** Reimbursable once per financial year (or per the applicable LTA block year, per Income Tax rules) on submission of travel bills, subject to tax-exemption rules for domestic travel; unclaimed LTA is paid out as a taxable component at year-end.
- **Meal Benefit:** ₹2,500/month, credited via meal card (tax-exempt as per Income Tax Rules, subject to prevailing exemption limits); unused monthly balance carries forward within the financial year but lapses at year-end.
- **WFH Setup & Internet Reimbursement:** One-time ₹15,000 setup allowance (desk, chair, monitor, etc.) plus ₹1,000/month internet reimbursement for employees confirmed as hybrid-eligible under the Work From Home Policy.
- **Learning & Development:** ₹25,000/year learning budget for courses, certifications, or conference attendance, subject to manager approval and relevance to the employee's current or aspirational role; unused budget does not carry forward to the next year.
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Free, confidential counseling sessions (up to 6/year), available to employees and immediate family, delivered through an external partner with no disclosure of usage to Nexora beyond aggregated, anonymized utilization statistics.
- **Mobile & Internet Reimbursement (non-WFH roles):** Employees in client-facing or field roles requiring regular mobile connectivity may claim up to ₹1,000/month against a personal mobile bill, subject to role eligibility as tagged by HR.
- **Relocation Assistance:** For employees relocating at Nexora's request (e.g., inter-office transfer), a one-time relocation allowance and temporary accommodation support (up to 30 days) may be provided, approved case-by-case by the Business Unit Head and HR.

## 5. Claim Process

- Health insurance and reimbursement claims are submitted via the HR portal, with supporting bills/receipts uploaded at the time of submission.
- Standard turnaround time is 10–15 working days from submission of complete documentation; incomplete submissions are returned to the employee with a specific query and the clock restarts on resubmission.
- Cashless hospitalization claims are coordinated directly between the hospital and the TPA and do not require employee-side claim submission, though employees should retain discharge summaries for personal records.
- Disputed or rejected claims can be escalated to the HR Benefits team, who will liaise with the insurer/TPA on the employee's behalf; escalations are acknowledged within 5 working days.

## 6. Annual Review

- All benefit sums insured, allowances, and reimbursement caps are reviewed annually by the People & Operations and Finance teams and may be revised effective the following financial year; employees are notified of any changes at least 30 days before they take effect.
