# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Attendance Policy

## 1. Work Hours

- **Working days:** Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are non-working days company-wide, except for roles explicitly designated for weekend coverage (e.g., production support, event staffing), which are compensated per Section 4 below.
- **Standard hours:** 9 hours/day, including a 1-hour lunch break (8 working hours net).
- **Core hours:** 10:30 AM – 4:30 PM — all employees, whether in-office or on an approved WFH day, must be online/present and reachable during this window, consistent with the Work From Home Policy.
- **Flexible start:** Any time between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM, based on when you clock in; the corresponding clock-out time is expected to maintain the 9-hour workday (e.g., a 9:00 AM clock-in implies an approximate 6:00 PM clock-out, inclusive of lunch).
- Employees whose role requires a fixed shift (e.g., night-shift support for international clients) follow a shift roster communicated separately by their manager and are exempt from the standard flexible-start window.

## 2. Marking Attendance

- Attendance is marked via **biometric check-in** at the office or the **Nexora HR app** on approved WFH days — both check-in and check-out are mandatory for the day to be marked complete.
- A missing check-out (e.g., forgotten punch) results in the day being flagged as "incomplete" in the HRMS until regularized.
- **Missed punches** must be regularized within **3 working days** via the HR portal, with manager approval; regularization requests older than 3 working days require additional HRBP sign-off and a documented reason.
- Employees traveling between office locations, or attending an external meeting/client site during work hours, should mark "on duty" for that portion of the day rather than leaving attendance incomplete.
- Attendance data feeds into monthly payroll processing and compliance reporting; discrepancies not resolved before the payroll cutoff (typically the 25th of each month) may result in a delayed correction the following pay cycle rather than the current one.

## 3. Late Coming

- Arriving after 10:30 AM without prior manager approval is marked as **"late."**
- Up to **3 late marks per month** are allowed without formal action, recognizing occasional commute or personal-emergency delays.
- **4 or more late marks in a month** trigger an automated flag to HR and the employee's manager; the manager is expected to have an informal check-in conversation to understand the cause.
- Repeated late-coming patterns (4+ late marks in 2 or more consecutive months) may be documented as a point of discussion in the employee's performance review and, if unaddressed, escalated to a formal conversation under the performance management process.
- Pre-approved late arrivals (e.g., a scheduled medical appointment, a client call in a different time zone) should be flagged to the manager in advance via chat/email and are not counted toward the monthly late-mark total, provided the manager confirms approval in the HR portal.

## 4. Overtime & Compensatory Off

- Employees required to work on a declared public holiday or a weekend (per their role's operational needs) are eligible for a **Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)**, to be availed within **30 days** with manager approval; unavailed Comp-Off lapses at the end of the 30-day window and is not encashed, consistent with the Leave Policy.
- The manager must tag the worked holiday/weekend day in the HRMS within 5 working days of the date worked for the Comp-Off to be validly credited.
- **Overtime pay** (where applicable, per role grade — typically applicable to certain non-exempt operational and support roles, not standard for Engineering/Product/Design/Sales grades) is processed through payroll on submission of approved timesheets, at a rate defined in the employee's role-grade compensation guidelines.
- Comp-Off and overtime pay are mutually exclusive for the same worked day — an employee cannot claim both for a single instance of holiday/weekend work.

## 5. Unapproved Absence

- **3 or more consecutive working days** of unapproved absence, with no communication to the manager or HR, may be treated as **voluntary abandonment of employment** (see Employee Handbook, Section 9), following a documented notice sent to the employee's last known address and personal contact details.
- A single day of unapproved absence is typically addressed informally between the employee and manager; a pattern of repeated single-day unapproved absences (e.g., 3+ instances in a rolling quarter) may be escalated to HR for a formal conversation, independent of the "3 consecutive days" abandonment threshold.
- Employees unable to reach their manager directly (e.g., manager also unavailable) should notify their HRBP or use the emergency contact process communicated at onboarding.

## 6. Business Travel

- Attendance is auto-marked as **"on duty"** for approved business travel (client visits, conferences, inter-office meetings, training) — no separate leave application is needed.
- Business travel must be pre-approved by the manager via the Travel & Expense portal before the trip, except in genuine last-minute client-driven scenarios, which should be regularized within 2 working days of travel.
- Travel days that extend into a weekend or holiday (e.g., a Friday-to-Monday client trip) do not automatically earn Comp-Off unless the employee is required to actively work (not merely travel) on that weekend/holiday day, subject to manager confirmation.
- International travel additionally requires HR and Finance sign-off for visa, insurance, and per-diem processing, as detailed in the Travel & Expense Policy.
