# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Dress Code Policy

## 1. General Principle

Nexora follows a **smart-casual, role-appropriate dress code**. The guiding principle is professional judgment: dress in a way that is comfortable, respectful of colleagues and clients, and suitable for the work you're doing that day, rather than following a rigid uniform.

## 2. Standard Office Days

- **Smart casual** is the default: collared shirts/t-shirts, trousers, chinos, jeans (without rips/tears), skirts, and dresses of a professional length are all acceptable.
- Sportswear, gym clothes, sleeveless vests (for any gender), flip-flops, and clothing with offensive graphics or language are not permitted in the office.
- Closed-toe footwear is recommended on factory-floor or hardware-lab visits for safety reasons, even for employees not permanently based there.

## 3. Client-Facing & External Meetings

- Employees attending client meetings, investor meetings, or external conferences (in person or on video) are expected to dress in **business casual** attire — collared shirts, blouses, blazers optional, and neat, pressed clothing.
- Sales, Customer Success, and Solutions Engineering teams should default to business casual on any day client interaction is scheduled, regardless of whether it's a Team Anchor Day or a WFH day.

## 4. Fridays / Casual Days

- Fridays are designated **"Casual Fridays"** company-wide — plain t-shirts, sneakers, and casual jeans are acceptable, provided no client-facing meeting is scheduled that day.

## 5. Work From Home Video Calls

- Employees are expected to dress appropriately (no sleepwear) for internal video calls, and business casual for any external/client video call, even when working from home.

## 6. Company Events & Branded Merchandise

- On company town halls, annual day, or team offsite events, employees are encouraged (not mandated) to wear Nexora-branded T-shirts or merchandise, distributed periodically by the People & Operations team.

## 7. Religious & Cultural Attire

- Nexora respects and accommodates religious, cultural, and traditional attire (e.g., turbans, hijabs, bindis, traditional festive wear) at all times, including client-facing settings, without requiring prior approval.

## 8. Exceptions & Special Roles

- **Facilities, Security, and Front-Desk staff** wear a company-issued uniform, provided and laundered by Nexora, for role identification and safety purposes.
- **Hardware-lab and on-site technical roles** may require safety gear (closed shoes, ID badge visibly worn, and in some cases safety vests) as mandated by the specific lab's safety guidelines.

## 9. Non-Compliance

- Repeated dress-code violations are addressed informally by the employee's manager in the first instance; a pattern of non-compliance after a documented conversation may be escalated to HR.
- Managers have discretion to send an employee home to change only in cases of clear safety-gear non-compliance in hardware-lab settings, not for general smart-casual judgment calls.
