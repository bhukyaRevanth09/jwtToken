# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Handbook

_Last updated: January 2026_

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Nexora Technologies! This handbook is a quick-reference guide to who we are, how we work, and what you can expect as part of the team. It works alongside our detailed policies — Leave Policy, Work From Home Policy, Benefits Policy, Attendance Policy, and Recruitment Policy — this handbook gives the overview, those documents give the specifics. Wherever this handbook and a detailed policy appear to conflict, the detailed policy document takes precedence.

## 2. About Nexora

- Nexora Technologies is a software product and services company with offices across India, headquartered out of its primary Hyderabad campus with additional offices in Bengaluru and Pune.
- We build tools for small and mid-sized businesses and operate a hybrid-first model across Engineering, Product, Design, Sales, and Operations.
- Nexora is organized into five broad functions: **Engineering & Product**, **Design**, **Sales & Customer Success**, **People & Operations**, and **Finance & Legal** — each led by a function head reporting into the leadership team.
- Our stated values — **Ownership, Candor, Customer-First, and Craft** — inform how performance is evaluated and how conflicts of process are resolved.

## 3. Employment Basics

- **Working days:** Monday to Friday; Saturdays and Sundays are non-working days company-wide except for roles explicitly designated as requiring weekend coverage (e.g., production support rotations), which are compensated per the Leave Policy's Compensatory Off provisions.
- **Standard working hours:** 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM (includes a 1-hour lunch break); core collaboration hours are 10:30 AM – 4:30 PM, consistent with the Work From Home Policy.
- **Probation period:** 3 months from date of joining, extendable by another 3 months at manager discretion if performance is inconclusive at the first review.
- **Confirmation:** A formal confirmation letter is issued on successful completion of probation, following a documented review between the manager and HRBP; confirmation also activates eligibility for Internal Job Postings, the Restricted Holiday selection, and full WFH hybrid eligibility.
- **Employment categories:** this handbook applies to full-time employees; contractors, interns, and consultants are governed by their individual engagement contracts, which take precedence over this handbook for those individuals.

## 4. Code of Conduct

- Treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and professionalism; discrimination or harassment on the basis of gender, religion, caste, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic will not be tolerated.
- Disclose any conflict of interest (financial or personal) to your manager and HR — this includes outside employment, consulting engagements, board positions, or close personal relationships with a direct reporting line (manager-report, or approver-approvee in finance/procurement).
- Company assets (laptop, software licenses, access credentials) are for official use — report loss or theft to IT within 24 hours; repeated or negligent loss may result in recovery of replacement cost per the IT Asset Policy.
- Confidential company and client information must not be shared externally, during or after employment, and remains subject to the confidentiality terms of the employment contract even post-separation.
- Gifts from vendors or clients above ₹2,500 in value must be declared to the Finance & Legal team; cash gifts of any value are not permitted.
- Use of company systems, email, and network access is subject to monitoring for security and compliance purposes, as disclosed at the time of onboarding.

## 5. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

- Nexora maintains a **zero-tolerance policy** toward workplace harassment, in line with the POSH Act, 2013.
- An **Internal Committee (IC)**, comprising employee representatives and an external member, handles all complaints confidentially and is empowered to recommend disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- Complaints can be raised via posh@nexoratech.example, directly to any IC member, or through your HR Business Partner; a complaint may be filed within 3 months of the incident (extendable by the IC in justified cases).
- The IC is required to complete its inquiry within 90 days of a complaint being filed, per statutory timelines, with interim measures (e.g., reporting-line changes, work-from-home arrangement) available to the complainant during the inquiry.
- Retaliation against a complainant or witness is treated as a separate, serious misconduct issue.

## 6. Leave & Time Off

- Full details are in the **Leave Policy**. In brief: 18 days Annual/Earned Leave, 6 days Casual Leave, and 6 days Sick Leave per calendar year, plus maternity leave (26 weeks for the first 2 children, 12 weeks thereafter), paternity leave (10 working days), and bereavement leave (5 days for immediate family) as applicable.
- Public holidays follow the annually published **Holiday List**, which varies slightly by function (see the Leave Policy's role-wise tables) and by office location.

## 7. Work From Home

- Nexora follows a hybrid model — see the **Work From Home Policy** for eligibility, weekly WFH days, Team Anchor Days, and equipment support.
- New joiners work from office during their 3-month probation period; hybrid eligibility begins at confirmation.

## 8. Compensation & Benefits (Overview)

- Compensation structure (fixed, variable, and retiral components) is detailed in individual offer letters and the Compensation Philosophy document, not reproduced here.
- Group health insurance covers the employee and immediate dependents (spouse, up to 2 children, and parents on an opt-in top-up basis); enrollment details are managed via the Benefits Policy.
- Employees are enrolled in the Employee Provident Fund (EPF) as per statutory requirements; gratuity is payable per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, on completion of 5 years of continuous service.
- Annual performance-linked variable pay and any ESOP grants are governed separately by the Performance Management and ESOP Policy documents respectively.

## 9. Grievance Redressal

- Raise any concern about your manager, team, or working conditions with your HRBP directly, or escalate anonymously via the **Ethics & Grievance Portal**.
- All grievances are acknowledged within 3 working days and are expected to reach a resolution or documented status update within 30 days.
- Grievances involving a conflict of interest with the assigned HRBP (e.g., a complaint about the HRBP themselves) are automatically routed to the next-level HR leader.
- Nexora's Whistleblower Policy governs disclosures related to financial irregularities, fraud, or serious ethical violations, and provides confidentiality protections distinct from the general grievance process.

## 10. Performance Management

- Performance is reviewed on an annual cycle (with a mid-year check-in), assessed against role-specific goals and Nexora's four core values.
- Ratings feed into compensation revisions, promotion eligibility, and — where relevant — the Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) process for employees rated below expectations.
- Employees placed on a PIP are ineligible to apply for Internal Job Postings until successful PIP closure.

## 11. Exit Process

- **Standard notice period:** 30 days (Individual Contributors), 60 days (Managers and above); notice period buyout by either party is governed by the terms in the Hiring Process / Offer documentation and is subject to manager approval.
- **Full and final (F&F) settlement** is processed within 45 days of the last working day, covering pending salary, leave encashment (per the Leave Policy), and any deductions (e.g., unreturned assets, notice period shortfall).
- HR conducts an **exit interview** for all separating employees, and feedback is aggregated anonymously for leadership review.
- Rehire eligibility is assessed case-by-case at the time of a former employee's reapplication; employees separated for cause (e.g., conduct violations) are generally marked ineligible for rehire.
- Relieving letters and experience certificates are issued within 7 working days of F&F settlement completion.

## 12. Acknowledgment

By continuing your employment with Nexora, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this handbook and agree to comply with the policies referenced within it. This handbook is reviewed and reissued periodically; the version in effect is the one published on the HR portal at any given time, regardless of any printed or previously downloaded copy.
