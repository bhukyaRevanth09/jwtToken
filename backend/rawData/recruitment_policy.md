# Nexora — HR Policy & Process Handbook

## 1. Hiring Process

1. **Application / Sourcing**
   - Candidates can apply via the public careers page, third-party job portals (LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed), or the Employee Referral Program.
   - Every application is logged in the Applicant Tracking System (ATS) with a unique candidate ID, source tag, and role code.
   - Walk-in and campus hiring drives follow a separate fast-track process managed by the Campus Relations team.

2. **Resume Screening**
   - The Talent Acquisition (TA) team screens resumes against the role's must-have and good-to-have criteria defined in the Job Requisition Form (JRF).
   - Screening SLA: resumes are triaged within 3 business days of application.
   - Candidates who don't meet minimum criteria are moved to "Not Progressing" status and receive an auto-generated email; TA may tag strong profiles for future roles in the Talent Pool.

3. **Screening Call**
   - A 20–30 minute call with a recruiter covering role expectations, current compensation, notice period, location/work-mode fit, and basic motivation questions.
   - Recruiters log call notes in the ATS, including a recommendation (Proceed / Hold / Reject).

4. **Technical Rounds**
   - 1–2 rounds with the hiring team depending on role seniority and complexity.
   - Entry-level and mid-level roles: typically 1 technical round (60–90 minutes) covering core skills and a practical exercise or case study.
   - Senior/lead/architect roles: 2 rounds — one hands-on technical round and one system design / strategy round, often with a panel.
   - Interviewers submit structured scorecards (rating + written feedback) within 24 hours of the interview.

5. **HR / Culture Round**
   - Conducted by the HR Business Partner (HRBP) for the relevant function.
   - Covers compensation expectations, culture fit, values alignment, and any logistical constraints (relocation, notice period, background check concerns).
   - For leadership roles (Manager and above), this round may be combined with a discussion involving the skip-level manager.

6. **Offer Rollout**
   - Offer is contingent on approvals from the hiring manager, HRBP, and (for roles above a defined band) the Business Unit Head.
   - Compensation bands are checked against internal parity guidelines before the offer is finalized.
   - Verbal offer is communicated first, followed by a formal offer letter within 2 business days of acceptance.

**Typical time-to-offer:** 2–4 weeks from the first interview, depending on role seniority, number of rounds, and panel availability. Leadership hires (Director and above) may take 6–8 weeks due to additional approval layers.

### 1.1 Interview Feedback & Decision Rules
- A candidate needs a "Proceed" recommendation from all interview panels to move forward; a single "Reject" at any technical round ends the process unless overridden by the hiring manager with written justification.
- Feedback must be submitted before the next round is scheduled — recruiters are expected to hold subsequent rounds until scorecards are in.
- Candidates are not given detailed interview feedback beyond a general reason for rejection (e.g., "technical fit," "experience level").

## 2. Internal Job Postings (IJP)

- Open roles are posted internally for **5 working days** before external sourcing begins, giving current employees first opportunity to apply.
- **Eligibility:** Employees with 12+ months in their current role, no active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP), and a "Meets Expectations" or higher rating in their last review cycle.
- The employee's current manager is notified automatically at the time of application — there is no requirement to seek prior manager approval before applying.
- Internal candidates skip the initial screening call but go through the standard technical and HR rounds, evaluated against the same bar as external candidates.
- If selected, the employee's transition timeline (typically 2–4 weeks) is negotiated between the current and future manager, balancing handover needs.
- Internal moves do not reset the employee's tenure-based benefits (leave balance, ESOP vesting schedule, etc.).
- Employees may apply to a maximum of 2 open IJPs at a time.

## 3. Employee Referral Program

- **Referral bonus:**
  - ₹25,000 for standard roles.
  - ₹50,000 for specialized or hard-to-fill roles, as tagged by Talent Acquisition (e.g., niche engineering skills, senior data science, security roles).
- **Payout structure:** 50% disbursed on the referred candidate's joining date (via payroll, taxable as per applicable slab), remaining 50% on the candidate completing 6 months of continuous employment.
- Referrals are submitted through the HR portal with the candidate's resume and the referrer's relationship/context note.
- **Eligibility exclusions:** Direct reports and immediate family members (spouse, parents, siblings, children) of the referrer are not eligible for referral bonus payout, though they may still apply through standard channels.
- A referral is considered "active" for 90 days; if the candidate is hired after 90 days from submission without further engagement, the referral may need to be resubmitted.
- Employees on notice period or those who have received a final warning are not eligible to raise new referrals.

## 4. Offer & Background Verification

- All offers are contingent on successful **Background Verification (BGV)**, conducted via a third-party agency, covering:
  - Education verification (degree, institution, year of passing).
  - Employment history verification (previous employers, tenure, role, reason for leaving).
  - Criminal record check (as permitted by local law).
  - Address verification, where applicable.
- BGV is typically initiated after offer acceptance and completed within 15–20 business days; joining may proceed with a "BGV in progress" flag, but continued employment is contingent on clearance.
- Any discrepancy found during BGV (e.g., misrepresented education, undisclosed employment gaps) is escalated to HR and may result in offer withdrawal or termination, even post-joining.
- Offer letters include:
  - Full CTC breakup (fixed, variable, benefits, retirals).
  - Notice period buyout terms, if applicable.
  - Probation period terms and duration.
  - Reporting manager and designated work location.

---

## 5. Notice Period Buyout

- Nexora may buy out an incoming candidate's notice period at its discretion, **capped at 30 days' equivalent pay**.
- Buyout is subject to business need (e.g., urgent project start) and requires approval from the hiring manager and HR.
- The buyout amount is typically paid by the candidate to their previous employer directly; Nexora does not reimburse buyout costs paid to a prior employer as a matter of default policy — any exception requires HR Head approval.
- Partial buyouts (e.g., reducing a 60-day notice to 30 days) are evaluated case by case.

## 6. Onboarding

- **Pre-boarding:**
  - Laptop and equipment are shipped to the new hire's address, or kept ready at the office, at least 3 days before the joining date.
  - New hire receives a welcome email with joining logistics, Day 1 agenda, and a document checklist (ID proof, education certificates, previous employment documents, bank details).

- **Day 1:**
  - HR orientation covering company policies, code of conduct, and benefits enrollment.
  - IT setup: email account, system access, and tool provisioning (Slack/Teams, HRMS, project management tools).
  - Buddy assignment — a peer from the same team who supports the new hire informally through the first 30 days.
  - Facility tour (for office-based roles) or virtual onboarding session (for remote hires).

- **First week:**
  - Meetings with immediate team members and key stakeholders.
  - Role-specific systems access and training scheduled based on function.

- **30/60/90-day check-ins:**
  - Structured conversations with the manager and HRBP during the probation period to review progress, address concerns, and confirm role fit.
  - 30-day check-in: settling-in feedback, access/tooling issues resolved.
  - 60-day check-in: performance against initial goals, additional training needs identified.
  - 90-day check-in: formal probation confirmation review; manager submits a recommendation (Confirm / Extend Probation / Terminate) to HR.

- **Probation period:** typically 3 months for individual contributor roles and up to 6 months for managerial or senior roles, extendable once by up to 3 months if performance is inconclusive.
